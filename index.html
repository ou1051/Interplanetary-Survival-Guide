Mission & sources

Plan a route from the habitat, drive to science stations and monitor your oxygen for the return. The Delta traverse uses fictional terrain. Gale Crater uses archived HiRISE elevation and imagery with a Curiosity field reference. Hazards, weather and life support are simulated.

The Mars globe streams NASA and USGS imagery and elevation. Coverage and resolution depend on the source.

Open-source inspiration

We studied Gale Marswalk Planner for terrain-aware route planning, Sol-3 for first-person exploration and source-linked learning, and NASA AMMOS 3D Tiles Renderer for multiscale 3D terrain architecture.
