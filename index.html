THE MISSION / THE EVIDENCE

Explore with curiosity.
Navigate with context.

This experience connects an astronaut’s journey to a Mars survival map. After landing, follow the 3D rover route, inspect four terrain layers, stop at hazard gates and complete science tasks while monitoring oxygen, energy and your return margin.

01 · JOURNEY

Imagine the arrival

The rocket, cockpit, Mars and landing are original illustrative reconstructions. Earth uses a credited Three.js example texture. The 84-second journey compresses a trip that would take months.

02 · SURFACE

Rehearse the decisions

The local rover terrain, hazards and environmental readings are synthetic. They demonstrate planning trade-offs and are not surveyed terrain or current Mars weather.

03 · ORBITAL MAP

Follow the evidence

The separate 3D Mars globe streams NASA/USGS imagery and elevation. Coverage and resolution depend on each source and network availability.

Open-source inspiration

We studied Gale Marswalk Planner for terrain-aware route planning, Sol-3 for first-person exploration and source-linked learning, and NASA AMMOS 3D Tiles Renderer for multiscale 3D terrain architecture. Their application code and assets are not copied into this journey. The Earth texture is from the Three.js examples (MIT); see repository asset credits.

Built with Three.js and CesiumJS. Independent Space Apps project; no NASA endorsement. Concept demonstrator, not operational astronaut navigation.
